# Artifact Perks — Tablet of Ruin (Heresy)

Only one artifact can be equipped at a time. Every artifact perk in a build must come from this one file.

Source: Destiny Data Compendium, `Artifact Perks` tab. Numbers in `[brackets]` are Crucible/PvP values; `↑` marks enhanced perk values.

*Arc | Void | Strand | Prismatic ⯁ Fusion Rifles | Glaives | Trace Rifles*

### Hold the Line

While within 15 meters of 3 enemies, while a Glaive or Machine Gun is readied:
+? Stability and +30 Reload Speed for Glaives and Machine Guns.
Glaive Projectile/Melee and Machine Gun Kills restore 55 HP.
Stat Bonus persist for 5 seconds once criteria isn''t met.
Healing on Kill does not persist.

### Vile Weave

Tangles inflict sever for 10+5 seconds upon dealing damage.
On Tangle Pickup:
Decreases the Tangle Cooldown by 4 second.
Can trigger from Moirai's Threaded Spike hitting a Tangle.
Can trigger from harvesting a Tangle from Luzaku's Teeming Nest.

### Particle Reconstruction

(Linear) Fusion Rifle hits inflict an unique stacking weaken that increases (Linear) Fusion Rifle damage.
5% | 10.25% | 15.8% | 21.6% | 27.6%
30% Weaken overrides Particle Reconstruction weaken.
Divinity's Cage also overrides it down to 15%.
Scoring multiple direct hits refills 10% of Magazine, rounded up.
Fusion Rifles: Every bolt in the magazine generally.
Aggressive Fusion Rifles (+Cull's Shadow) uniquely trigger every other burst.
Precision Linear Fusion Rifles: Magazine + 2
Adaptive Burst Linear Fusion Rifles: 3 x (Magazine + 4)
Bastion triggers every 7 bursts.
Euphony Threadlings count for hits, otherwise 30 hits. (Triggers in 8 bursts)
Delicate Tomb triggers every 3 full bursts. Jotunn triggers after 8 shots.
Mythoclast triggers every 25 hits

### Dielectric

On Arc-Debuffed Enemy Kill:
Grants x1 Bolt Charge.
Unlike Data Disk's version, this does NOT spawn Orbs nor restore health on Arc Debuffed Rapid Kills.

### Maligned Harvest

Upon inflicting Volatile 4 times within 5 seconds of each:
The next Void Damage Instance within 10 seconds releases a weakening burst, affecting enemies within 7 metres.
Upon inflicting Weaken on a Non-Weakened Enemy:
+25 Void Overshield HP for 10+5 seconds.
Only triggers once per source of weaken.
Eg: Smoke Bomb won't repeatedly trigger it.
Does not grant Void Overshield with Fafnir or Tractor Cannon.

### Elemental Supercharger

On Exhausted or Severed Enemy Kill with Super-Matching Weapons:
Grants 2?% Additional Super Ability Energy.

### Volatile Marksman

Upon scoring multiple Precision Hits within 3 seconds of each, or upon scoring 3 kills within 3 seconds of each with Void Weapons:
Grants Volatile Rounds for 10 [6] seconds.
Precision Hits trigger for Volatile Rounds:
(25% of Magazine Size) + 1, rounded down.
Bows: 2
Triggering a Volatile detonation grants 10% Class Ability Energy.

### Flashover

Upon reaching x10 Bolt Charge:
Amplified for 15 seconds.
Bolt Discharges deal 50% increased damage against Combatants.

608 Impact + 406 Splash = 1014 Flashover

### Heavy Ordnance Regeneration

Upon scoring 8 Machine Gun damage instances, or 2 Rocket Launcher damage instances within 7 seconds of each:
25% [5%] Damage Resist (Resist x2) for 15.5 seconds
85% Additional Base Grenade and Melee Ability Regeneration Rate for 10 seconds.
While Heavy Ordnance is active:
Elite+ Combatants Kills disorient Rank-and-File Combatants within 10 meters.

### Unraveling Orbs

Unraveling Rounds for 14 [?] seconds.

### Horde Shuttle

Dealing Weapon Damage equal to 10% [100? HP] of an Unraveled Enemy's Combined Health + Shields spawns a Threadling.
Incurs a 0.5 second cooldown between Threadlings.
Threadlings inflict sever upon dealing damage.

Grants x10 Bolt Charge.

Upon triggering a Bolt Discharge:
Restores ~55 HP.
Begins Critical Health regeneration
Does not begin recharging Shields.

### Elemental Siphon

Upon scoring 3 kills within 3 seconds of each using a Kinetic Weapon or a Super-Element Matching Weapon:
Creates an Elemental Pickup that matches the equipped Super Ability's element.
Upon collecting an Elemental Pickup:
Grants 5% [1%] Element-Matching Super Ability.
Incurs a 2 second cooldown between additional Ability gains.

### Harsh Refraction

Upon scoring 3 Trace Rifle hits against enemies affected by Element-Matching Debuffs:
Grants increased Trace Rifle damage for 4 seconds.
Legendary Trace Rifles = 50% increased damage
Exotic Special Traces / Lodestar = 35% increased damage
Microcosm deals 20% increased damage after scoring 4 hits against enemies affected by ANY Elemental Debuff.

Void = ↓Suppression ↓Volatile ↓Weaken

### Perpetual Destruction

Non-Exotic Rocket Launcher Kills within 2-3? seconds of each progress a counter.
Rank-and-File = 25% | Elite = 34% | Miniboss = 100%
Upon reaching 100% Counter Progress:
Rocket Launchers are granted +1 Ammo.
Precision/High-Impact Rockets (or Bipod RLs) receive +2 Ammo.
+? Reload Speed and 0.?x Reload Duration Multiplier for 10 seconds.
Not affected by Scavenger Mods.

### Gold from Lead

Upon picking up 5–6 Special Ammo Bricks:
Generates 8% Heavy Ammo, rounded up.
Pickup Counter is reset on death.
Not affected by Scavenger Mods.
Does not function in the Crucible.
