# Weapon Perks, Mods, and Traits — Glaive Information

Source: Destiny Data Compendium, `Weapon Perks` tab. Numbers in `[brackets]` are Crucible/PvP values; `↑` marks enhanced perk values.

### Melee Attacks

[Melee] while a Glaive is readied lunges towards enemies up to 7 meters away, dealing 227 [97.04] damage.
Can be chained into a 3-hit combo with a 0.467s delay between hits. Animation delay after combo ends can be skipped by [ADS]
The final hit of the combo deals 78.5% [88.8%] increased damage.
Glaive Melee Attacks are treated as Kinetic Damage Unpowered Melee Attacks.
Weapon Damage Buffs don't buff Melee Attacks. Doesn't benefit from Titan's Melee Damage Intrinsic.

### Projectile Attacks

Glaive Projectile Attacks are treated as Weapon Damage, and will benefit from any generic or weapon-specific damage increases.
Prevents Melee Attacks for 0.2 seconds after shooting.

### Weapon Energy & Shield

Scoring a Ranged Projectile Hit grants 25% Weapon Energy by default.
Glaives passively generate 1% Weapon Energy per second while readied.
[ADS] with a Glaive projects a frontal shield that grants Damage Resist against attacks that strike it, at the cost of draining Weapon Energy and a Movement Speed Penalty.
Base Glaive Block Movement is normally 0.85x without Weapon Energy, and 0.66x with Weapon Energy.
The time to raise the shield is based off of Handling and ADS Speed values.
Glaive Shield:
97.5% Damage Resist against Combatants.
50% Damage Resist against Guardians. Primary Ammo Weapons and Melee Attacks are only granted 30% Damage Resist.
Vexcalibur uniquely has 50% Damage Resist against all damage from Guardians.
Weapon Energy Drain Rate:
0 SD = 6.5s | 50 SD = 12s | 100 SD = 18? seconds
Drain Rate = 6.5 + (Shield Duration x ~0.1)
