Weakspot Hits that do not register as a Precision Hit can still benefit or trigger Weakspot effects, such as Headseeker, or Heavy Burst Hand Cannon's intrinsic 9.5% Weakspot Damage Buff.
Similarly, Precision Hits are not always scored on Weakspots, and may not trigger Weakspot effects.
One such example are Shielded Combatants. Weakspot Damage Increases will work when other Precision Hit Increases wouldn't.
