### Area Denial Frame

Shoots a burst of 5 projectiles at the cost of 1 Ammo.
Projectiles create a lingering damage field after 0.5 seconds of impacting.
Damaging Field deals damage every 0.25 seconds for 5.65 seconds, dealing 52.5 [?] damage up to 22 times per field.

### Balanced Heat Weapon

Firing generates Heat equal to the Heat Generated stat.
Passively dissipates Heat equal to the displayed Cooling Efficiency value each second, even while stowed.
Reaching 1000 Heat triggers Overheat, forcing a Manual Vent and rendering the weapon unusable until its Heat is ≤150.
Upon beginning a Manual Vent while Heat is ≥875:
2x Vent Rate Multiplier.
Primary Ammo Balanced Heat Weapons intrinsically deal 20% Increased Impact Damage to Elites, Mini-bosses, Bosses, and Vehicles.
Additional information regarding Heat Weapons.

### Double Fire

Fires 2 rounds at a slight offset from the reticle at the cost of 1 Ammo.

### Dynamic Heat Weapon

Firing generates Heat equal to the Heat Generated stat.
Passively dissipates Heat equal to the displayed Cooling Efficiency value each second, even while stowed.
Reaching 1000 Heat triggers Overheat, forcing a Manual Vent and rendering the weapon unusable until its Heat is ≤150.
Upon cancelling a Manual Vent while Heat is ≤125:
Dynamic Heat Limiter | -50% Heat Generation for 5 seconds or until stowing the weapon.
Triggering Dynamic Heat Limiter through reload or sprint after having dealt damage will freeze the timer until shooting.
Primary Ammo Dynamic Heat Weapons intrinsically deal 15% Increased Impact Damage to Rank-and-File, and 38% Increased Impact Damage to Elites, Mini-bosses, Bosses, and Vehicles.
Additionally, they deal 2.5% increased direct damage to Guardians.
Additional information regarding Heat Weapons.

### Support Frame

Shoots slow, tracking projectiles that travel at a speed of 40m/s. Projectiles do not have a distance limit, but will eventually lose their tracers.
Has 50% Increased Aim Assist Falloff Distance at 0 Range, down to 25% at 100 Range, compared to a regular Auto Rifle.
Dealing ANY (really, ANY!) damage generates Weapon Energy, at a rate of 1% Energy for every 4 [3.6] damage dealt, reaching 100% Charge after dealing 400 [360] damage.
Dealing damage with the Support Auto Rifle doubles the Weapon Energy gains, generating 1% Energy for every 2 [1.8] damage, requiring 200 [180] damage to fully charge.
Looking near an ally within range widens the reticle. Hipfiring while an ally is in healing range will shoot healing projectiles that have much stronger tracking.
Consumes 2% Weapon Energy per healing shot fired towards an ally, restoring 13.3 HP to them on impact.
Targeting Range = 28 + (0.1 x Range Stat) meters, up to 38 Meters at 100 Range.
If multiple allies are targeted, then the healing shots will alternate between allies.
Upon scoring 5 healing hits within 1 second of each on allies:
The next healing hits within 1 seconds of each on allies bestows them with an Element-Matching Effect, and applies Support Frame Boost to the user and the targeted ally.
Support Frame Boost grants 10% increased weapon damage for 6 seconds. Stacks with everything.
Arc Support Frames bestow Amplified for 7 seconds to the targeted ally.
Solar Support Frames bestow Restoration x1 for 4+2 seconds to the targeted ally.
Void Support Frames bestow 15 Void Overshield for 10+5 seconds to the targeted ally.
Strand Support Frames cause the targeted ally to release 4 Unraveling Projectiles.
Stasis Support Frames bestow a stack of Frost Armor for 5 seconds to the targeted ally.
Only triggers On-Heal Effects upon applying Rapid Healing. Shots that restore missing health count for "on-hit" related interactions.
Incurs a 1.5 second cooldown upon applying the Support Frame Boost before additional healing hits count towards the Support Frame Boost on the same ally.
