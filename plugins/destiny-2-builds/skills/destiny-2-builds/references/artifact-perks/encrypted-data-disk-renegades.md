# Artifact Perks — Encrypted Data Disk (Renegades)

Only one artifact can be equipped at a time. Every artifact perk in a build must come from this one file.

Source: Destiny Data Compendium, `Artifact Perks` tab. Numbers in `[brackets]` are Crucible/PvP values; `↑` marks enhanced perk values.

### Kinetic Synthesis

Dealing Kinetic Weapon Damage and scoring Kinetic Weapon Kills progress a counter, spawning an unique Kinetic Ammo Brick upon reaching 100%.
Counter lasts 15s and is refreshed upon dealing Kinetic Weapon Damage.
Each damage point grants 0.0265% progress, requiring 3770 damage to spawn a brick.
A499 generates 75% less progress (15K damage for a brick).
RnF Kill = 8% | Elite = 15% | Miniboss = 25%
Damage requirement IGNORES Power-Delta, but considers ACTUAL DAMAGE DEALT.
This means it requires the same "base damage" regardless of reduced damage from PL deltas.
Upon collecting a Kinetic Ammo Brick:
+5% Special and +5% Heavy Ammo Progress.
Refills equipped Kinetic Damage Weapons and generates Special/Heavy Ammo for them.
Generates (1/3rd of Magazine) + 1 Ammo, with certain exceptions.
Counts for Ammo Pickup effects. Kinetic Ammo Generation is unaffected by Scavenger Bricks.
Bastion = +1 Ammo | Cull's Shadow = +2 Ammo | Praxic = +2 Ammo | Microcosm = +12 Ammo.

### Kinetic Rupture

Upon scoring enough Kinetic Weapon Damage Instances against Elite+ Combatants:
Spawns a Kinetic Rupture for ? seconds which counts as a Weakspot.
Kinetic Ruptures can be damaged, activating after 0.3? seconds of being dealt damage, stunning Unstoppable Champions and dealing damage based on the amount of damage it received.
Kinetic Rupture Damage doesn't count as dealing damage to Combatants, uses PVP values for Damage Buffs, doesn't count as hitting nor missing either.
Eg: Radiant is 10%, Surges are 3% | 4.5% | 5.5%, Collective Action is 11%.
It's complicated, not worth on 99% of situations.
Tends to decrease damage by 25% to 50% on almost every situation.
One of the few meaningful exceptions is Izanagi x4
Izanagi x4 is +120% damage down baseline to +63% based on the buffs used.

### Precision Equity

Precision Kills grant stacks of Precision Equity, up to 10 stacks.

Consumes all stacks to spawn a MASSIVE Orb of Power.

On FIRMAMENT OF POWER PICKUP:
Weapon Surge x2 for 11 seconds.
Does not overwrite stronger Weapon Surges.
Works for all damage types.

### Sticker Shock

Flux, Fusion, and Magnetic deal increased damage on direct hits.
Flux = 60% | Fusion = 30% | Magnetic = 22.5% total increase
Only the first Magnetic explosion gets increased by 45%.
Does not increase the Solar Effect Damage of Fusion Grenades.
Upon scoring a kill with the aforementioned grenades:
Spawns 4 homing Kinetic Micro-Missiles over the Enemy Death Location.
Micro-missiles track the closest enemy within 30 meters.
Micro-Missiles deal 216 [?] Kinetic Damage on impact.
They're identical in behavior to Ancillary Ordinance's missiles.
