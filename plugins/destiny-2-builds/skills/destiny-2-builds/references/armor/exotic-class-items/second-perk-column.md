# Exotic Class Item Perks — Second Perk Column

An exotic class item rolls one perk from the first column and one from the second. Both perks must be legal for the class: class-agnostic, or from that class's own section.

Source: Destiny Data Compendium, `Exotic Class` tab. Numbers in `[brackets]` are Crucible/PvP values; `↑` marks enhanced perk values.

Contents: Class-agnostic Perks · Hunter-exclusive Perks · Titan-exclusive Perks · Warlock-exclusive Perks

## Class-agnostic Perks

### Spirit of the Star-Eater

Picking up an Orb of Power while Super is charged grants a stack of Feast of Light, up to a maximum of x6 stacks.
Swapping to another Exotic Class Item with Star-Eaters will maintain the current Feast of Light stacks.
On Super Cast:
Consumes all Feast of Light stacks, increasing Super Damage for 14 seconds.
18.2% | 36.4% | 54.6% | 59.7% | 64.8% | 70% increased Super Damage.
Reduced effectiveness with certain Super Abilities.
Song of Flame does not receive increased Solar Effect Damage.
Nova Bomb, Thundercrash, and Twilight Arsenal receive up to 50% Increased Super Damage.
Needlestorm receives up to 35% Increased Super Damage.

### Spirit of the Synthoceps

While within 15 metres of 3 enemies:
Grants Biotic Enhancements for 5 seconds.
Biotic Enhancements:
165% [100%] Increased Melee Damage.
100% Increased Glaive Melee Damage.
Decreased effectiveness with Lightning Surge against Guardians.

### Spirit of Verity

Upon scoring a Grenade-Element Matching Weapon Kill:
Grants a stack of Death Throes, up to 5 stacks, increasing Grenade Ability Damage and granting Bonus Grenade Ability Energy per second per stack.
Loses 1 Death Throes stack upon expiring, rather than all of them.
Death Throes stack timer begins decaying after 2 seconds of gaining a stack.
Death Throes:
x1 = 40% [20%] increased Grenade Damage, +0.5% Bonus Grenade Energy/s for 8 seconds.
x2 = 55% [40%] increased Grenade Damage, +1% Bonus Grenade Energy/s for 7 seconds.
x3 = 70% [60%] increased Grenade Damage, +1.5% Bonus Grenade Energy/s for 6 seconds.
x4 = 85% [80%] increased Grenade Damage, +2% Bonus Grenade Energy/s for 5 seconds.
x5 = 100% increased Grenade Damage, +2.5% Bonus Grenade Energy/s for 4 seconds.

## Hunter-exclusive Perks

### Spirit of the Coyote

Grants an extra Class Ability Charge.

### Spirit of the Cyrtarachne

On Grenade Ability Usage:
Grants Woven Mail for 10 seconds.

### Spirit of the Gyrfalcon

Upon leaving Void Invisibility:
Grants Volatile Rounds for 9 [3] seconds.

### Spirit of the Liar

Receiving Melee Damage, or scoring a Powered Melee Hit grants Cross Counter for the next Melee Hit within 3 seconds.
Cross Counter grants 400% increased Basic and Arc Melee Damage.
Cross Counter's Melee Attack deals Arc Damage.

### Spirit of the Wormhusk

On Class Ability Usage:
Grants x2 Cure.
Upon reaching 100% Counter Progress by scoring multiple Kills within 3 seconds of each:
Rank-And-File = 34% | Elite+ and Guardians = 50%
Grants a stack of Burning Souls for 10 seconds, up to a maximum of 3 stacks.
All stacks expire at once. Retriggering Burning Souls refreshes the buff duration.
Burning Souls: Class Ability Usage healing is enhanced and spreads to allies within 15? meters:
x1 = x3 Cure.
x2 = x1 Cure + Restoration x1 for 3 seconds.
x3 = x2 Cure + Restoration x1 for 5 seconds.
If Restoration is active, then it will extend its duration by the corresponding duration.
