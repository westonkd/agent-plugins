# Artifact Perks — NPA Repulsion Regulator (Deep)

Only one artifact can be equipped at a time. Every artifact perk in a build must come from this one file.

Source: Destiny Data Compendium, `Artifact Perks` tab. Numbers in `[brackets]` are Crucible/PvP values; `↑` marks enhanced perk values.

*Arc | Void | Strand ⯁ Glaive | Auto Rifles*

### Improved Unraveling

Unraveling Threads deal 15% increased damage.

### Strand Soldier

Upon receiving Woven Mail, while on a Strand or Prismatic Subclass:
Grants Unraveling Rounds for 8 seconds.

### Conductive Cosmic Needle

Against Strand-Debuffed Boss Combatants:
5% Increased Arc and Void Ability Damage per Strand Debuff.
1x = 5% | 2x = 10.25% | 3x = 15.76%

### Volatile Flow

On Orb of Power or Void Breach Pickup:
Volatile Rounds for 9 [?] seconds.

### Suppressing Glaive

Upon dealing a Glaive Damage Instance against Combatants:
Inflicts Suppression for 10 seconds.
Glaive Melee inflicts Suppression at the cost of 10% Glaive Energy.
Does not drain Glaive Energy if the enemy is suppressed.

### Shock and Awe

While an Arc or Prismatic Subclass is equipped, while Amplified:
Arc Kills trigger a lightning burst, dealing up to 126 [?] Arc Damage and jolting in a 8 metre radius.
Incurs a 5 second cooldown between activations.

### Unto the Breach

On Void Debuffed Enemy Kill while on a Void or Prismatic Subclass:
Spawns a Void Breach.

### Protective Breach

On Void Breach Pickup:
+45 Void Overshield for 10+5 seconds.

### Supernova

On Void Breach Pickup:
The next Void Damage Instance within 10 seconds releases a weakening burst, inflicting Weaken to enemies within 5? metres for 8 seconds.

### Amped Up

While on Arc or Prismatic Subclass:
Amplified duration is increased to 20 seconds.

### Counter Charge

Upon stunning a Champion:
Grants a stack of Armor Charge.

### Squad Goals

On Finisher while affected by the Super-Matching Elemental Buff:
Allies within ? metres are bestowed the Subclass-Specific Buff.
Subclass Specific Buff:
Arc: Amplified | Void: Devour | Strand: Woven Mail

### Thunderous Retort

While at Critical Health or Amplified:
30% increased Arc Super Damage.
Only affects damage while in the Super Animation, resulting in lowered bonuses for Thundercrash and Gathering Storm.

### Bricks from Beyond

Every 3? Elite+ Void Weapon Kills:
50?% Heavy Ammo Progress to the user and allies within ? meters.

### Lightning Strikes Twice

On Arc Grenade Ability Usage:
130% Additional Grenade Base Recharge Rate for 5 seconds.
Arc Kills increase the buff duration by +3? seconds, up to 20 seconds.

### Sustained Fire

Upon scoring 10 Auto Rifle Hits within 1.5 seconds of each against a single Combatant:
Resist x2 (25% DR) for 6 seconds.
Buff is refreshable and persists through stow.
Support Autos can also trigger the buff by healing allies.

### Overload Grenades

Void Grenade Ability Damage inflicts disruption.

### Passive Aggressive Guard

While a Glaive is readied:
50% Damage Resist against Combatants within ? meters.

### Shatter Orbs

Upon first breaking a Combatant Shield:
Creates an Orb of Power that grants 7.15% Super Energy.
Performing a Finisher on a Combatant that didn't trigger the effect will also spawn an Orb of Power.
Only works once per enemy.

### Targeting Autoloader

Auto Rifle Combatant Kills progress a Counter.
Kills grant 50% Counter Progress.
Kills while within 15 meters of 3 enemies grants 100% Progress.
Kills while at 5 stacks grant 100% Progress.
Counter Progress persists through stow.
Upon reaching 100% Counter Progress:
Grants a stack of Targeting Autoloader for 15 seconds, up to a maximum of 5 stacks.
Buff is refreshable.
Targeting Autoloader:
Refills 20% | 20% | 30% | 40% | 40% of Magazine
10% | 13% | 16% | 18% | 20% Increased Auto Rifle Damage.
Stacks with other buffs.
Buff is removed upon readying a non-Auto Rifle Weapon.

### Void Weapon Channeling

Upon scoring a Void Weapon Kill while having at least 1 charged Void Ability:
Grants Weapon Surge stacks equal to the amount of Void Grenade, Melee, Class, and Super Ability Charges for 11 seconds.
Up to x4 Weapon Surge by having 4 Void Ability Charges.
2 Grenade + 2 Melee = x4 | Grenade + Melee + Class + Super = x4
Buff can only be refreshed if the stack amount is less or equal (≤) to the amount of Void Ability Charges.
Works as expected on Prismatic!
